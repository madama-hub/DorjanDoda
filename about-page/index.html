Episode I THE CODE AWAKENS Dorjan is a physics student with a strong passion for space, coding, and computers. Driven by curiosity and a love for understanding how things work, he explores both the laws of the universe and the systems that power modern technology.

Alongside his studies in physics, he builds apps, experiments with code, and constantly expands his knowledge of computers and digital creation. For him, programming is more than a skill — it is a way to turn ideas into reality.

Inspired by science, technology, and the endless sense of possibility found in space, he is building his path one project at a time. This portfolio is part of that journey.
